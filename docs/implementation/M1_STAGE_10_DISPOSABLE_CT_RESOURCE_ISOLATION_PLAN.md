## Faults, rollback and controlled cleanup

- Insufficient capacity, unavailable template, incomplete cluster visibility, CTID collision or unavailable trusted admin route: stop before creating anything; no fallback or bypass.
- Provision/copy or hash mismatch, missing tools, unexpected network/interface/mount, template anomaly or unbounded resource usage: stop testing, contain the guest via authorized administrator, preserve sanitized state, and obtain a decision; do not modify any other guest or host settings.
- Invalid sudoers or failed operator/recovery test: keep independent admin access; restore the saved known-good **guest** policy through the proven console and validate before another operator test. If recovery cannot be proven, stop; do not improvise changes to host/CT200. Any pointer-related ambiguous activation result is inspected; no blind rollback or destructive migration.
- At end of the specifically authorized window, an administrator must receive **explicit cleanup authorization** before stopping/destroying the exact CT and its own root disk and guest-local synthetic material. First verify node, CTID, name and volume ownership; never use blanket purge or remove shared templates, unrelated storage, backups or other guests. Record sanitized evidence required for review before deletion. Verify the exact CT and its owned volume are absent afterwards and no other resource was selected. If cleanup approval is withheld or identity is ambiguous, leave the guest isolated, report its state/ongoing resource consumption and ask for a decision.
