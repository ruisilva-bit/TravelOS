# Stage 10 — DEVELOPMENT signing custody preparation

**Design contract only; mutable, not installed, not approved by an independent reviewer.** The Product Owner (PO) is Signing Key Custodian and recovery authorizer; Hermes is technical implementer. Receipt Verification Authority and independent human Privacy Review Authority are **PENDING**, not named or appointed. AI delegates under the same operational control do not establish independence. Preparation-only decision: no definitive key generation, provisioning, privileged host change, service restart, release promotion, CI/freeze or Stage 11. This proposal does not accept the DEVELOPMENT signing exception: `M1_STAGE_10_DEVELOPMENT_SIGNING_DECISION.md` requires independent adjudication. Production remains keyless, unchanged.

## Boundary and custody mechanism

Use a separate, DEVELOPMENT-only Cosign signing key on an independently controlled signing executor, not in the artifact, checkout or CT200 trust registry. The PO controls creation, encrypted backup and recovery authorization. A future fixed one-shot service autonomously signs only approved immutable artifact digests; approval of the service policy is distinct from per-request manual approval. Its dedicated service identity can read only its own scoped credentials and fixed inputs and write only its output; the requesting identity cannot read credentials, modify executable, service, policy, pin, ledger or output, or become that identity/root. Hermes must never receive or access the definitive key, passphrase or equivalent decryption capability. Existing broad sudo invalidates that separation until removed by separately authorized and independently verified cutover; a different UID or `systemd` unit alone does not establish it. Host root and credential provisioning authority are trusted and outside this boundary; compromise of either compromises signing. There is no claim of protection against malicious root, kernel, service binary or authorized signer compromise.

Preferred candidate: a protected Cosign encrypted private-key credential delivered via `LoadCredentialEncrypted=` and a separately protected passphrase credential, both decrypted only for the fixed service, subject to an actual CT200/systemd/TPM-or-host-key protection assessment and Cosign credential interface qualification. Systemd encrypted credentials' protection and portability depend on the selected encryption mode, host key/TPM, host root and backup model; they are not an HSM, an independent authority, or automatically portable. Determine exact pinned Cosign and systemd versions and verify whether the selected Cosign interface can consume both credential files without exposing passphrases in argv, logs or an environment readable by unauthorized principals. If it cannot, select and independently review another protected signer/interface; do not invent a wrapper, export `COSIGN_PASSWORD` indiscriminately, or use a plaintext disk file. The Cosign key's own encryption and the systemd encrypted credential layer are separate controls. Credential creation/provisioning and all recovery operations are performed by PO-controlled trusted personnel, never by Hermes.

A qualified service must use a pinned, protected executable and dependencies, a fixed non-shell entry point and no caller-selected commands, paths, environment, key, trust roots or interpreter; no generic sudo, Python, Node or shell. Harden with no network, restricted namespaces/capabilities, read-only system and narrowly scoped writable directories, resource and timeout bounds; verify these controls against actual OS/version. Service invocation grants signing operation, not credential access. Do not create an installed unit from this design until independently reviewed and separately authorized.

## One-shot contract to implement and review

The following is a normative design, not an existing service or validator. A protected independently administered policy identifies exactly DEVELOPMENT/CT200, pinned signer/service/tool versions and hashes, approved source/build qualification authority and policy revision, active key fingerprint, request schema and size limits. It must be validated from protected fixed locations, never chosen by caller. Policy approval must bind the source/build qualification authority separately from the signing identity; a signature alone is not provenance. Missing, stale, unreadable, revoked or untrusted policy, revocation state, trusted time, credentials or authorization fails closed.

The fixed one-shot receives a bounded request containing exactly: `schemaVersion`, `environment` (`DEVELOPMENT`), `instance` (`CT200`), `requestId`, `artifactId`, `artifactSha256`, `sourceInputSha256`, `qualificationReceiptSha256`, `policyVersion`, `keySha256`, `notBefore`, `expiresAt`. Reject duplicates, unknown/missing fields, bad encodings, noncanonical timestamps, wrong scope, expiry, future authorization and malformed lowercase SHA-256. `requestId` identifies one immutable authorization, not a filename or command. The authenticated qualification must bind every request field, be issued under an independently provisioned trusted qualification authority, and be independently checked against trusted time and revocation; caller-supplied PASS or hash alone is not authorization. An appointed Receipt Verification Authority is necessary where the applicable evidence authority contract requires one; no fabricated authority is permitted.

The service obtains the artifact from a fixed ingress, safely snapshots a bounded regular file using no-follow/anchored traversal and immutable sealed bytes, and recomputes `artifactSha256` on the **same bytes** passed to Cosign. Reject symlinks, race/identity changes, unsafe types, limits and mismatches. Consume credentials only inside the restricted signer. Sign the sealed bytes using fixed, reviewed Cosign parameters; independently verify the returned detached bundle against the independently pinned public-key file and exact sealed artifact before publishing. Offline transparency must be explicitly represented as `OFFLINE_NO_TRANSPARENCY_PROOF`, never OIDC or transparency assurance. Reject missing, invalid or altered signature, altered artifact, unpinned key and DEVELOPMENT/production mismatch. Signatures cannot bypass migration, restore, qualification, activation or independent review gates.

Serialize attempts and persist a durable, exclusive claim binding `requestId` to all request and authorization hashes **before** signing. A reused request ID with different bindings rejects. On crash, partial output, fsync/readback failure or unknown outcome, quarantine the attempt; do not assume failure, return success or sign again automatically. After inspecting protected state, a trusted operator may return an existing exact output only after independent re-verification and readback; otherwise issue a newly authorized request under the policy. Write signature and sanitized receipt to protected temporary output, fsync, atomically publish without replacement, fsync parent and read back/verify exact bytes. Validate supported filesystem semantics and crash/restart/concurrency cases on the real storage before relying on them. Neither service nor signing receipt publishes canonical evidence or supplies its own independent authority.

## Independent public pin and lifecycle

1. After independent review and separate real-operation authorization, PO-controlled personnel generate the definitive key outside Hermes access, using a qualified version and protected credential channel. PO separately authenticates the exact public key bytes and lowercase SHA-256 of those bytes through an independent channel and retains a versioned, access-controlled off-CT200 registry. Registry record includes environment, key ID/fingerprint, exact public-key bytes or immutable location, algorithm, version, validity, status, predecessor, approval reference and authenticated custodian confirmation. A key distributed only alongside its artifact is not a trust anchor.
2. An independent authorized provisioner verifies the registry over a separately authenticated channel, then installs a corresponding protected verification policy. Record who verified which exact bytes, registry version, fingerprint, destination, time and method. Another independently controlled readback compares installed exact bytes and registry; self-comparison or root ownership is insufficient. The existing activation policy's `publicKeySha256` is SHA-256 of exact public-key **file bytes**, not a key ID or claim of provenance.
3. Maintain encrypted, access-controlled off-instance backups, with separate recovery material and PO-controlled access. Inventory locations, versions, retention and integrity without recording secrets. Do not copy credential ciphertext and its only decryption key together. Qualify portability of host-bound systemd credentials; if not portable, use a separately encrypted recoverable export produced by PO-controlled personnel. Recovery occurs only after PO authorization into a quarantined, access-separated environment; restore encrypted material, reconstruct an operational protected credential without exposing plaintext to Hermes, verify its public fingerprint against the independent registry and perform a disposable signed-challenge/verification drill. Record a non-secret outcome and identity; clean transient material. A design is not a proven recovery.
4. Rotation: generate a distinct new DEVELOPMENT key under the same custody rules; authenticate and independently register its public bytes; approve a versioned transition with exact start/end, allowed key IDs and rollback semantics. Provision and independently read back new verification pins before directing signing to the new key. Sign only authorized bytes; test old/new, wrong-profile and out-of-window cases. Existing activation code's single pin is not a dual-key rotation mechanism; either use a separately reviewed policy migration or implement/qualify an explicit overlap before relying on one. Retire old signing ability and, after the approved window, remove its authorization, preserving historical verification evidence. Never silently treat retired/revoked signatures as permission for new activation.
5. Suspected compromise/loss: stop signing and new activation, notify PO, independently record reason/time and revoke key and pending authorizations in the off-host registry. Distribute authenticated versioned revocation to signer and verifier through independently controlled protected provisioning; verify readback. Unknown/stale/unavailable revocation state blocks new signing and activation; current activation implementation does not yet implement this revocation feed. Preserve running release unless separate safe incident action is authorized; do not infer that historical signatures or prior release provenance are trustworthy. Assess affected signatures and recover with a new key only after review. No automatic unsigned fallback or destructive rollback.

## Appointment, authorization and receipt formats

These are formats for future authenticated records, not appointments or approvals. Exact-byte hashes use SHA-256 lowercase hex; timestamps are UTC RFC3339; IDs are opaque non-secret identifiers. Use strict versioned parsing, no duplicate/unknown fields, an authenticated envelope binding the exact payload bytes, and externally protected identity/trust anchors. A JSON shape or signature verified using a caller-supplied key does not authenticate a person or establish independence. The PO establishes candidates' identities, separate control/access and conflicts of interest out of band, records scope/validity and revocation, and obtains affirmative acceptance over an authenticated independent channel. No actual name, signature or record is asserted here.

**Appointment payload v1:** `schemaVersion`, `appointmentId`, `role` (`RECEIPT_VERIFICATION_AUTHORITY` or `PRIVACY_REVIEW_AUTHORITY`), `subjectId`, `human` (true required for privacy role), `appointingCustodianId`, `scope` (environment, instance, permitted decisions), `independenceAssessmentRef`, `authenticationMethodRef`, `validFrom`, `validUntil`, `revocationSourceRef`, `acceptedAt`, `acceptanceEvidenceRef`. Protect the appointment envelope and its immutable digest independently of Hermes and the signer. Verify validity, role, human requirement, revocation and independence before relying on any decision. A different label, AI delegate or mere signature is not proof of independent control.

**Signing operation receipt v1:** `schemaVersion`, `requestId`, `environment`, `instance`, `artifactId`, `artifactSha256`, `sourceInputSha256`, `qualificationReceiptSha256`, `policyVersion`, `policySha256`, `keySha256`, `bundleSha256`, `signerToolSha256`, `verificationResult`, `transparencyStatus`, `completedAt`. This is a sanitized operational output, not a qualification or independent acceptance. Protect receipt transport and retain exact bytes; no private material, passwords or customer data.

**Receipt-authority decision v1:** `schemaVersion`, `decisionId`, `appointmentSha256`, `authorityId`, `environment`, `instance`, `candidateId`, `sourceInputSha256`, `artifactSha256`, `receiptSha256`, `decision` (`ACCEPT` or `REJECT`), `issuedAt`, `expiresAt`, `revocationSourceRef`, `replayId`. Verify authenticated appointment, scope, exact bytes, clock and revocation; reject replay with different binding and unauthorized roles. `ACCEPT` is not privacy clearance.

**Privacy decision v1:** `schemaVersion`, `decisionId`, `appointmentSha256`, `reviewerId`, `environment`, `instance`, `candidateId`, `sourceInputSha256`, `artifactSha256`, `payloadManifestSha256`, `publicationMetadataSha256`, `classification`, `retentionPolicyRef`, `accessPolicyRef`, `decision` (`ACCEPT` or `REJECT`), `issuedAt`, `expiresAt`, `revocationSourceRef`. The human reviewer evaluates every exact retained/exposed byte and metadata. Changed content, metadata, retention/access terms or scope requires a new decision. Scanner output and synthetic reviewers do not equal clearance.
