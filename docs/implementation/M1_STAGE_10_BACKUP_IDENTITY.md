## Safeguards and failure states

Before any authorized change, inventory and protect the exact applicable pre-change configuration and identity state, prove independent administrative recovery, and create an authenticated encrypted snapshot for retention **off host**. Encrypt on CT200 to an independently pinned, non-secret public recipient; the private recovery key and protection secrets must remain off CT200, outside backup data, source and evidence. Verify ciphertext transfer, integrity and retention independently; ciphertext existence alone is not recoverability. Retain the encrypted snapshot; do not overwrite or delete it during cleanup. Host-loss key recovery is a separate, unqualified gate.

Only a future reviewed, fixed operator may carry out narrowly scoped changes after separate authorization. It must validate exact targets and preconditions, preserve protected configuration, verify peer/HBA effective behavior and unaffected access, and emit sanitized evidence without credentials or customer data. No restart is permitted; any configuration activation method and its impact require independent review and explicit approval. Do not improvise commands or bypass a denied executor.

Fail closed on missing approval, unverified recipient or off-host retention, ambiguous identity or configuration, path mismatch, HBA shadowing, unexpected privilege/membership, insufficient recovery access, or inability to verify the resulting state. Stop without claiming success; preserve evidence and retained ciphertext. Partial application, interrupted execution or lost process state is **unknown**, not success or safe rollback. Reconciliation and any recovery after process loss require a future separately approved executor; none is asserted to exist. Do not blindly retry, overwrite, remove access or restart services.

## Independent gates

1. **Design and authority:** independent security/operations review of exact fixed identity, two HBA rules and ordering, least privilege, socket resolution, no-restart activation and administrative recovery; separate owner authorization for the specific execution.
2. **Snapshot and custody:** independent review of exact snapshot scope, authenticated encryption, pinned off-host recipient, off-host private-key custody, verified retained ciphertext and recovery limitations. Separate approval and evidence are required for any actual key provisioning.
3. **Implementation and qualification:** review the exact operator bytes and failure/reconciliation design; qualify positive and denied peer access, privilege boundaries, pre-existing access and interruption cases in an authorized disposable environment. A design review or synthetic test is not CT200 execution evidence.
4. **Execution and adjudication:** only after the preceding gates and explicit target authorization, independently verify sanitized target-state and failure evidence and adjudicate open findings. Backup, native verification, restore, process-loss recovery and Stage 10 GO remain separate gates; none is established by identity bootstrap alone.
